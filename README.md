# WorldBar

A tiny world that lives on your Windows taskbar. A handful of people around a fire, left to work it out
for themselves — you watch, or play god if you must.

**Status: 0.1 alpha.** Expect rough edges; please report anything odd.

## Install

1. Go to **[Releases](https://github.com/Shaingan/WorldBar-releases/releases/latest)** and download `WorldBar-Setup-x.y.z.exe`.
2. Run it. Windows will probably say **"Windows protected your PC"** — that's because the game isn't
   signed with a paid certificate yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. WorldBar installs in a few seconds and starts on its own. Look at the bottom of your screen.

## Updates

The game checks for updates by itself. When one is ready, a green **⬆** appears on the toolbar —
click it and the game restarts on the new version. You can also check any time in **☰ → ⚙ → Check for updates**.

**Your world is saved automatically and survives every update** (it lives in `%APPDATA%\WorldBar\saves`,
with backups).

## Playing

- **Hover the strip** to see the controls. **Drag the toolbar** to move the world anywhere, even to another monitor.
- **Drag the land** to look around, **mouse wheel** to zoom, **click anyone** for their character sheet.
- **▾** shrinks it to a little globe you can park anywhere. **Ctrl+Alt+H** hides it completely.
- Uninstall from Windows Settings → Apps like any other program.
